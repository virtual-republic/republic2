*A commune exists only because the Assembly said so. This charter is made under
the measure named in the front matter, and is entered by the Registrar — Article
4 § 1 ². It cannot be formed as of right, and it cannot outlive the repeal of
the measure that made it.*

## § 1  Establishment

¹ The Commune of the North Bank is established under P-0012, carried by the Assembly, and entered on the register by the Registrar.

² It is a commune within the meaning of Article 4 § 1 ², and holds an account of the Republic.

³ It exists at the Assembly's pleasure. A measure repealing P-0012 dissolves it, and § 9 then applies.

## § 2  Purpose

¹ The commune is a standing body of citizens who have undertaken to do together what the Republic has not undertaken to do for them.

² Its purpose is stated in P-0012 and may not be enlarged by this charter.

## § 3  Membership

¹ Membership is open to any citizen, on a signed application to the convenor, and is not refused.

² **Every member is a coequal member.** A member admitted today stands exactly as a founder stands. There is no seniority, no weighted vote, and no class of member.

³ A member may withdraw at any time by a signed record, and may return.

⁴ A member may be excluded only by a resolution carried by two thirds, on stated grounds, after the member has been heard — Article 9 § 4 ¹.

## § 4  Decisions

¹ Each member has one vote, whatever they hold and however long they have been a member.

² A resolution carries when a third of the members vote and half the decisive votes are in favour.

³ The quorum in § 4 ² is deliberately low. A commune that cannot gather half its members should still be able to act; a member who does not vote is taken to be content.

⁴ Any member may propose a resolution. There is no gatekeeper.

## § 5  The convenor

¹ The commune acts through its convenor.

² The convenor is filled by a resolution of the kind *officer*, and holds the organ for one year. There is no limit on how many terms a member may serve, and no requirement that anyone serve.

³ The convenor MAY: receive applications for membership; call and record the commune's decisions; and spend from its account within a limit the members set each year.

⁴ The convenor MUST NOT: exclude a member; amend this charter; or commit the commune to anything beyond the limit in § 5 ³. Each of those is a resolution.

⁵ Where the office is vacant, the members act directly, and any member may call a resolution.

## § 6  Property

¹ The commune holds its account for its members in common. No member has a share in it, and no member takes anything from it on withdrawal.

² A commune may not issue instruments — Article 4 § 2 ³. It has no shares, and this charter cannot give it any.

## § 7  Openness

¹ Every decision of the commune is published in the Journal of the Republic, as every act is.

² The commune keeps no private record of a decision it has taken.

## § 8  Consistency

¹ This charter is subordinate to the Constitution and to P-0012, and any provision inconsistent with either is of no effect.

² Where P-0012 is amended, this charter is read as amended to conform.

## § 9  Dissolution

¹ The commune is dissolved by a resolution of two thirds of its members, by repeal of P-0012, or by judgment of the Court.

² On dissolution its holdings pass to the Treasury — Article 4 § 4 ². No member takes any part of them, which follows from § 6 ¹.
