*The front matter above is what the tools read. Everything below binds the
members as their own agreement. Altering the prose without altering the front
matter changes what the members owe each other and nothing else — § 4 ³.*

## § 1  Name and type

¹ The entity is named Schoonover Holdings.

² It is a company, formed under Article 4 § 1 ¹ by c-0001, and holds an account of the Republic.

³ It is not a citizen, does not vote in the Assembly, and holds no office — Article 4 § 2 ⁴.

## § 2  Purpose

¹ The company exists to hold property, to contract, and to distribute what it earns among its holders.

² It may do anything a company may lawfully do, and nothing this charter forbids.

## § 3  Shares

¹ The company issues instruments of the class *ordinary*, each conferring one vote and an equal share of any distribution.

² The company MAY issue a further class only by a resolution of the kind *charter*, which MUST state the rights of the new class and how it ranks against the existing ones.

³ Instruments held by the company itself confer no vote and receive no distribution.

⁴ The company is **not listed**. Its shares exist, are recorded in the register, and pass directly between accounts. They are not traded on the exchange.

⁵ Listing is opened by amending the `listed` field of this charter, which is a resolution of the kind *charter*. It cannot be opened by a decision of the director alone.

## § 4  Decisions

¹ Each holder votes in proportion to the instruments they hold.

² A resolution carries when half the vote is cast and half the decisive votes are in favour.

³ The figures in § 4 ² are those in the front matter of this charter. The tools read them there, and there only.

⁴ Every resolution is published with its ballots, and any holder may reproduce the count.

## § 5  The director

¹ The company acts through its director, and through no one else — Article 4 § 3 ⁴.

² The director is filled by a resolution of the kind *officer*, and holds the organ for one year.

³ The director MAY: contract on behalf of the company; transfer its holdings in the ordinary course of its purpose; place and cancel orders where the company is listed; and admit members where this charter provides.

⁴ The director MUST NOT: issue instruments; amend this charter; dissolve the company; or transfer more than a tenth of the company's holdings in any thirty days. Each of those is a resolution of the holders.

⁵ A director who acts beyond § 5 ³ acts without authority, and the act is of no effect — Article 1 § 2 ³.

## § 6  Distributions

¹ The holders MAY resolve to distribute the company's holdings among themselves in proportion to their shares.

² No distribution MAY be made that would leave the company unable to meet a contract it has executed.

## § 7  Transfer of shares

¹ A share passes by the signed act of its holder — Article 10 § 4 ².

² Where the company is not listed, a holder intending to transfer SHOULD first offer the share to the existing holders in proportion, on the same terms, for fourteen days. This paragraph confers no power to refuse a transfer; it is a courtesy the holders owe one another and the Court will not enforce it against a purchaser.

## § 8  Consistency

¹ This charter is subordinate to the Constitution, and any provision inconsistent with it is of no effect — Article 4 § 3 ³.

² It narrows what the type of company allows. It does not widen it, and could not.

## § 9  Dissolution

¹ The company is dissolved by a resolution of the holders taken by two thirds of the vote, or by judgment of the Court.

² On dissolution its holdings are distributed among the holders in proportion, and what cannot be so distributed passes to the Treasury — Article 4 § 4 ².
