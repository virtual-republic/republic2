## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act fixes the grounds referred to in Article 3 § 4 ², on which the Court may remove a citizen, and the procedure for the recall of an officer under Article 6 § 3 ³.

² Removal is the gravest act the Republic can take against a person. This Act makes it hard, slow and public, and leaves it with the Court.

## § 2  Grounds for removal

¹ The Court MAY remove a citizen only for conduct, and only where satisfied that the citizen has:

² persistently and knowingly published falsehoods about another person, after a finding under the statute on deliberation;

³ used a citizenship at another's direction, contrary to Article 2 § 6 ⁴;

⁴ obtained a citizenship by asserting an identity that is not theirs; or

⁵ acted to destroy or corrupt the register.

## § 3  What is never a ground

¹ No citizen is removed for an opinion, for disagreement, for criticism of an officer, or for proposing a measure that failed.

² No citizen is removed for inactivity. A citizen who never votes remains a citizen.

³ No citizen is removed for departing and returning.

## § 4  Procedure

¹ A removal begins on the application of a citizen, and is heard by the Court.

² The citizen complained of MUST be given notice, the evidence, and an opportunity to answer — Article 9 § 4 ¹.

³ The Court MUST NOT remove on the application alone, and MUST state its reasons in a published judgment.

⁴ A removal takes effect twenty-one days after publication, during which the citizen may appeal.

## § 5  Effect

¹ Removal marks the citizenship removed. It alters no record the citizen made — Article 3 § 4 ³.

² A removed citizen keeps a complete copy of the register — Article 9 § 9 ¹ — and this Act does not touch that right.

³ A removed citizen may apply for admission afresh after one year, and the application is decided by the Assembly.

## § 6  Recall of an officer

¹ Any citizen MAY propose the recall of an office-holder, by a measure of the class of the election that filled it.

² A recall measure names the office and the holder, and states its ground. It need not allege misconduct.

³ Where a recall carries, the office is vacant, Article 6 § 3 ⁴ applies, and an election follows within thirty days.

⁴ A recalled officer may stand again in that election.

⁵ Recall is not a finding against anyone. It is the Assembly withdrawing a trust it gave.
