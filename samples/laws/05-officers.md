## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act gives effect to Article 6 § 4 ³, by which the powers attaching to each office lapse unless re-approved by the Assembly each year.

## § 2  Annual approval

¹ In the first month of each year the Auditor SHALL lay before the Assembly a measure re-approving the powers of every office as they then stand.

² The measure states each office and its enumerated powers, and is of the class of ordinary measure.

³ Where the measure is not carried within sixty days, the powers of every office lapse, and Article 6 § 3 ⁴ applies until they are approved afresh.

## § 3  The Auditor's report

¹ The report required by Article 6 § 5 ³ SHALL state, for each office, every power exercised in the period and the record of its exercise.

² It SHALL state every occasion on which a check upon the Republic's procedure was suspended, disabled or circumvented — Article 6 § 5 ⁴.

³ It SHALL state whether the register verified on each day of the period.

## § 4  One person, many offices

¹ Where one citizen holds more than one office, the Auditor's report SHALL say so, and say which powers are thereby held together.

² The Assembly SHALL consider, on each annual approval, whether those powers ought to be separated.

³ This section is a duty to notice, not a prohibition. Article 6 § 3 ² permits the holding.
