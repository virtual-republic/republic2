## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act fixes the period referred to in Article 8 § 5 ², during which an enactment does not take effect and the Court may halt it.

² Until this Act, the period was unfixed, and so the Court's power to halt under Article 7 § 3 ¹ had no window in which to operate.

## § 2  The period

¹ An enactment takes effect seven days after its publication in the Journal.

² An enactment of the class of amendment or of entrenched provision takes effect twenty-one days after publication.

³ An election takes effect on publication, and this section does not apply to it.

## § 3  Halting

¹ The Court MAY halt an enactment within its period, on the application of any citizen.

² A halt is published, states its reasons, and suspends the enactment until the Court decides.

³ Where the Court does not decide within thirty days, the halt lapses and the enactment takes effect.

## § 4  Urgency

¹ The Assembly MAY, in the measure itself and by the threshold of the class above it, declare that an enactment takes effect on publication.

² A declaration of urgency MUST state the reason, and does not displace the Court's power to declare the act of no effect afterwards.
