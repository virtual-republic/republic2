## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act fixes the period referred to in Article 3 § 2 ⁴, within which the Registrar may object to an admission.

² Until this Act, the period was unfixed, and so the Registrar's power to object could not be exercised at all.

## § 2  Admission takes effect at once

¹ Admission takes effect on the recording of the application — Article 3 § 2 ³. Nothing in this Act delays it.

² A citizen admitted may vote, propose, stand and act from that moment.

## § 3  Objection

¹ The Registrar MAY object within seven days of the recording.

² An objection MUST state its ground, and MUST be one of: that the application was signed by a key already on the register, contrary to Article 2 § 6 ³; that the applicant has stated they are under sixteen; or that the same natural person has been shown to be operating the citizenship at another's direction, contrary to Article 2 § 6 ⁴.

³ An objection on any other ground is of no effect. In particular, no objection lies on any ground listed in Article 3 § 2 ⁵.

## § 4  Effect of an objection

¹ An objection suspends the citizenship until the Assembly decides.

² The Assembly decides by ordinary measure within thirty days. Where it does not, the objection lapses and the citizenship stands.

³ Acts done by the citizen before the objection was recorded stand.

## § 5  The Registrar's account

¹ The Registrar SHALL state in the Auditor's report every objection made, every ground relied on, and every outcome.

² A Registrar who objects on a ground not permitted by § 3 ² acts beyond their office — Article 1 § 2 ³ — and the Assembly may recall them.
