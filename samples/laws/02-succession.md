## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act gives effect to Article 3 § 3 ⁴, which provides that a citizen who has lost every key may be restored by the Registrar on such proof as statute requires.

² Until this Act, no such proof was fixed, and so no restoration was possible.

## § 2  Second keys

¹ Every citizen SHOULD register a second key, held apart from the first — Article 3 § 3 ³.

² A citizen holding two keys restores their own citizenship by rotating the lost key with the key retained, and needs nothing in this Act.

## § 3  Restoration on proof

¹ A citizen who has lost every key MAY apply to the Registrar to register a new one.

² The application MUST state the citizenship claimed and the new public key.

³ The Registrar SHALL grant it only where satisfied, on evidence recorded in the Journal, that the applicant is the person who held that citizenship.

⁴ Evidence sufficient for this section includes a signature made before the loss over a text the applicant now identifies, a record of the applicant's acts that only they could give, or the attestation of two citizens who are not the Registrar.

## § 4  Notice and objection

¹ An application is published on receipt, and MUST NOT be granted for fourteen days.

² Any citizen MAY object, and the Court decides an objection.

³ A restoration is published, stating the evidence relied on.

## § 5  Effect

¹ Restoration registers a new key to an existing citizenship. It creates no new citizenship and alters no record.

² Acts done with the lost key before its loss was published stand.
