## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act provides for the appropriation referred to in Article 10 § 6 ², and for the assessment referred to in Article 2 § 5 ³.

² Without it, nothing may leave the Treasury at all, and no assessment may be made against any account.

## § 2  Appropriations

¹ Nothing leaves the Treasury except under an appropriation carried by the Assembly.

² An appropriation states the amount, the account it goes to, and the purpose.

³ An appropriation is exhausted when the amount has been disbursed, and lapses at the end of the year in which it was carried.

⁴ The Treasurer disburses under an appropriation and not otherwise. A disbursement citing no appropriation is of no effect — Article 2 § 5 ³.

## § 3  Standing appropriations

¹ The Assembly MAY carry a standing appropriation, which does not lapse, for a recurring purpose it states.

² A standing appropriation MUST state a maximum for each year, and MUST be re-approved every three years or it lapses.

## § 4  Assessments

¹ An assessment is a debit of an account made without the holder's signature, and is permitted only by this section — Article 2 § 5 ³.

² An assessment MAY be made only:

³ against an entity, for a fee the Assembly has fixed by statute for a service the entity has taken; or

⁴ against any account, to give effect to a judgment of the Court.

⁵ No assessment MAY be made against a citizen's account for any other reason, and no assessment is ever made by an officer acting alone.

## § 5  Publication

¹ Every appropriation, every disbursement and every assessment is published.

² The Treasurer SHALL publish each quarter the balance of the Treasury, every appropriation outstanding, and every amount disbursed under each.

## § 6  Limits

¹ The Treasury MUST NOT hold an instrument of an entity, and MUST NOT trade on the exchange.

² The Republic MUST NOT give anything of value in return for the unit — Article 10 § 1 ³ — and this Act creates no exception.
