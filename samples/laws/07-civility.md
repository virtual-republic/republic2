## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act provides for the conduct of deliberation, and for what follows from its abuse.

² It is made under Article 9 § 3 ³, which withholds the protection of free expression from harassment, threats and deliberate falsehood about a person.

## § 2  Deliberation

¹ A citizen speaking to a measure addresses the measure.

² Disagreement, including sharp disagreement, is not misconduct, and this Act MUST NOT be read to make it so.

³ No citizen is answerable under this Act for an opinion, however unwelcome.

## § 3  Misconduct

¹ Misconduct under this Act is harassment of a citizen, a threat against a person, or a statement about a person made knowing it to be false.

² A complaint is made to the Court, which decides it after hearing the citizen complained of — Article 9 § 4 ¹.

## § 4  Remedies

¹ The Court MAY publish a finding, require a correction to be published beside the record complained of, or suspend a citizen's right to speak in the Assembly for a stated period not exceeding thirty days.

² The Court MUST NOT suspend a citizen's right to vote, to stand, or to depart.

³ Removal from the register is available only under Article 3 § 4 ², and only for conduct.

## § 5  Nothing is unpublished

¹ A record complained of is not removed — Article 9 § 3 ².

² A correction is published beside it, and both stand.
