## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act provides for the withholding permitted by Article 5 § 3 ², and limits it.

² The presumption of this Act is publication. Withholding is the exception, is temporary, and is itself published.

## § 2  Grounds

¹ A record MAY be withheld only where its publication would expose a natural person to a serious risk of harm.

² No record is withheld to spare an officer, an entity or the Republic embarrassment, criticism or liability.

³ No record of an act of the Assembly, the Court or an officer is withheld.

## § 3  Procedure

¹ Withholding is ordered by the Court, on application, for a stated period not exceeding one year.

² The order is published, stating the ground, the period, and the number of records affected.

³ The Court MAY renew an order once, on fresh application, and the renewal is published.

## § 4  Expiry

¹ On expiry the record is published without further act.

² The Keeper SHALL publish, each year, a list of every record withheld and every record released.

## § 5  Nothing is deleted

¹ A record withheld remains in the register and in the chain.

² Withholding conceals a record from publication; it does not remove it, and Article 2 § 2 is not thereby breached.
