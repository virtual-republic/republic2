## § 1  The method

¹ Orders on the exchange are matched by price and then by time.

² An order to buy is matched with an order to sell when the price offered is not less than the price asked.

³ Where several orders would match, the best price is taken first; and among orders at the same price, the one recorded first.

## § 2  The price

¹ A match is made at the price of the resting order, being the order recorded earlier of the two.

² The party who crosses the spread accepts the terms of the party who waited.

## § 3  Partial satisfaction

¹ Where an order is matched in part, the remainder of that order is cancelled.

² No order is left standing in part against the intention of the person who placed it.

³ A cancellation under this section is published as an act of the exchange.

## § 4  Standing orders

¹ An order that is not matched remains on the book until it is matched, cancelled by the person who placed it, or lapses under statute.

² The book is published in full before each settlement.

## § 5  Dealing for oneself

¹ An order is not matched with another order of the same account.

² No officer MAY place an order in knowledge of an unpublished order of another.
