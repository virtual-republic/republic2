## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act provides for the appeal referred to in Article 7 § 4 ⁴.

² Until this Act, a judgment of the Court was final in fact, whatever Article 7 § 4 ⁴ said, because no route lay from it.

## § 2  Who may appeal

¹ A person affected by a judgment MAY appeal it within twenty-one days.

² A person is affected who was a party, or whose act, holding, deed or citizenship the judgment touches.

## § 3  Where an appeal lies

¹ Where the Court sits with more than one Judge, an appeal lies to the Judges who did not decide.

² Where the Court sits with one Judge, or where every Judge decided, an appeal lies to the Assembly.

³ The Assembly hearing an appeal decides by ordinary measure, and MUST state reasons. It does not thereby become the Court.

## § 4  Grounds

¹ An appeal lies on the ground that the Court construed a provision wrongly, that it decided without hearing the appellant, or that it exceeded its powers under Article 7 § 3.

² An appeal does not lie merely because the appellant disagrees with the outcome.

## § 5  Effect

¹ An appeal suspends a judgment that halts or voids an act, unless the Court orders otherwise.

² The appeal body MAY affirm the judgment, reverse it, or return it to the Court with directions.

³ Every appeal and every outcome is published with reasons.

## § 6  Finality

¹ There is no appeal from an appeal.

² A question once decided is not reopened, except by a measure of the Assembly changing the law for the future, which does not disturb what was decided.
