## § 1  Purpose

¹ This Act provides for deeds — recognised title to property, a claim, a right, a licence or a membership.

² A deed is valid only when the Keeper of the Journal has recognised it and it is published in the Journal.

## § 2  Requests

¹ Any citizen MAY ask the Keeper to recognise a deed, stating what is claimed, who is to hold it, and whether it may be transferred.

² A request confers nothing and is not title.

³ The Keeper SHALL decide a request within thirty days, and a refusal states its reasons.

## § 3  Recognition

¹ The Keeper MAY recognise a deed on request or on their own motion.

² The Keeper SHALL NOT recognise a deed which would purport to grant what the Republic does not have, or which is inconsistent with the Constitution.

³ Recognition and publication are one act.

## § 4  Transfer

¹ A deed is transferable only if it says so.

² A transferable deed passes by the signed act of its holder, and the register records every holder it has had.

³ A deed that is not transferable stays with the person or entity to whom it was granted, and lapses when they depart or are dissolved.

## § 5  Challenge

¹ Any citizen MAY put the validity of a deed to the Court.

² The Court MAY declare a deed of no effect, and the declaration is published.

³ A deed declared of no effect is marked so in the register. The record of its recognition is not removed.
