Obol issuance was carried by the Assembly and is enacted by publication in this issue — Article 8 § 5 ¹.

Of 1 ballot cast against an electorate of 1, 1 were in favour and 0 against.

The text in force is stat.obol-issuance. It is amended only by a further measure, and every version is recorded there.
