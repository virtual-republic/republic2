Commune Act was carried by the Assembly and is enacted by publication in this issue — Article 8 § 5 ¹.

Of 2 ballots cast against an electorate of 2, 2 were in favour and 0 against.

The text in force is stat.commune-act. It is amended only by a further measure, and every version is recorded there.
