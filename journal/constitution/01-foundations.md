## § 1  The Republic

¹ The Republic is a voluntary civic association of its citizens.

² It is not a state, claims no sovereignty and holds no territory.

³ Citizenship confers no status under the law of any state and alters no obligation of any person.

⁴ It belongs to no ancestry, faith or tradition.

## § 2  Authority

¹ All authority resides in the citizens.

² Authority is exercised only where this Constitution, or an instrument made under it, confers it.

³ No office, organ or entity holds authority not conferred, and none MAY confer authority it does not hold.

## § 3  Supremacy

¹ This Constitution is the whole of the Republic's authority and the limit of it.

² An act inconsistent with it is of no effect.

³ Its provisions are mandatory and prohibitory unless expressly declared otherwise.

## § 4  Form

¹ This Constitution establishes institutions, allocates authority and guarantees rights.

² No provision of it MAY fix a rate, a sum, a period, a threshold or the detail of a procedure. Those belong to statute and to the settings the tools read.

³ A provision of that character, however enacted, takes effect as statute.

## § 5  Language

¹ The language of the Republic is English, and the English text is authentic.

² A translation MAY be published for convenience. It is not authentic and confers no right.

## § 6  Interpretation

¹ The Preamble forms part of this Constitution. It confers no right and imposes no duty.

² The keywords MUST, MUST NOT, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD and MAY are used as defined in RFC 2119.

³ This Constitution is read purposively, and so as to apply to circumstances its framers did not foresee.

⁴ Where a provision bears more than one reading, the reading that better secures the rights in Article 9 prevails.
