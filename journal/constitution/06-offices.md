id: art-06
title: Offices
---

## § 1  The offices

¹ The offices of the Republic are the Registrar, the Keeper of the Journal, the Treasurer, the Auditor and the Judges of the Court.

² Statute MAY create further offices.

## § 2  The Assembly

¹ The Assembly is all citizens.

² The Assembly makes statutes, elects to office, approves expenditure and amends this Constitution.

³ It acts by the procedure in Article 8 and by no other means.

## § 3  Tenure

¹ An office is held for the period fixed by the settings, and is filled by election under Article 8.

² A citizen MAY hold more than one office.

³ An office-holder MAY be recalled by the procedure for their election.

⁴ On vacancy the Assembly appoints until an election is held.

⁵ An office recorded to a citizenship that is not active is vacant in fact.

## § 4  Powers

¹ Every office holds an enumerated set of powers and no others. The set is published.

² Every exercise of a power is recorded, with the office under which it was exercised.

³ The powers attaching to each office SHALL be re-approved by the Assembly at least once a year, and lapse if they are not.

## § 5  Accountability

¹ Public office is a public trust.

² No officer MAY use office for private gain, or accept anything of value that compromises the independent exercise of official judgment.

³ The Auditor SHALL report to the Assembly at least twice a year, and the report is published.
