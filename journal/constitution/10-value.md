---
id: art-10
title: Value
---

## § 1  The unit

¹ The unit of account of the Republic is fixed by the settings.

² The unit has no value outside the Republic. It MUST NOT be sold, purchased, redeemed, exchanged for anything of value, or represented as an investment.

³ No person MAY give anything of value to obtain it, and the Republic MUST NOT give anything of value in return for it.

## § 2  Issue

¹ The unit is issued only by the Treasurer, only under a resolution of the Assembly that has carried, and only in the amount that resolution states.

² An issue exceeding the cap fixed by the settings is of no effect.

³ Every issue is recorded and published.

## § 3  Accounts and transfer

¹ Every citizen, and every entity whose type allows it, MAY hold an account.

² A transfer is a record and takes effect when recorded.

³ A transfer is signed by the holder of the account debited, or by an organ acting for it.

## § 4  Instruments

¹ An entity whose type allows it MAY issue instruments representing a share in itself.

² An instrument is transferable between accounts and is recorded in the register.

³ Section 1 ² applies to instruments as it applies to the unit.

## § 5  Exchange

¹ The Republic maintains an exchange for the transfer of instruments.

² The exchange clears by periodic auction at a uniform price, and gives no priority to the order of arrival within a period.

³ Every order, every clearing and every price is published.

## § 6  Treasury

¹ The Treasury holds the account of the Republic.

² Nothing leaves the Treasury except under an appropriation carried by the Assembly, executed by the holder of the power to disburse.
