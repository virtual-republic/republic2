id: art-02
title: Invariants
entrenched: true
---

*These hold at all times and are enforced by the tools of the Republic. An act
that would violate one does not fail; it does not occur.*

## § 1  Attribution

¹ Every record MUST identify exactly one author.

² The author is a citizen, or an organ of an entity acting through a citizen.

³ A record whose author cannot be verified is not received.

## § 2  Immutability

¹ A record, once written, MUST NOT be altered.

² A correction is a new record referring to the record corrected.

³ The removal of personal data under Article 9 § 8 removes no record.

## § 3  Continuity

¹ Every record MUST carry the hash of the record before it.

² The Republic SHALL publish, at intervals of not more than one week, a signed checkpoint attesting the state of the register.

³ A checkpoint MUST be verifiable by any person, without permission and without an account.

## § 4  Citation

¹ Every act MUST cite the provision under which it is made.

² An act citing no provision, or one that does not resolve, is not received.

## § 5  Conservation

¹ Value is created only by the organ holding the power to issue, under Article 10.

² A transfer neither creates nor destroys value.

³ No account MAY be debited except by its holder, by an assessment made under statute, or by order of the Court.

## § 6  Plurality

¹ A citizenship is a civic person, not a natural person.

² One natural person MAY hold one or more citizenships. Each is distinct, acts separately and votes separately.

³ Each citizenship MUST hold a key no other citizenship holds.

⁴ Citizenship MUST NOT be sold, lent or transferred.

⁵ The Republic makes no claim that its citizenships correspond one to one with natural persons, and no count of citizenships is a count of persons.
