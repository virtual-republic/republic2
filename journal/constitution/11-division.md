---
id: art-11
title: Division
---

*A disagreement that cannot be resolved within the Republic is resolved by
division. This Article states in advance how, so that division is an act of the
Constitution and not a failure of it.*

## § 1  The right

¹ Any citizen MAY declare a division, stating the ground and the name the departing Republic will take.

² No organ MAY prevent a division, delay it, or impose a condition upon it.

## § 2  Effect

¹ On division the declaring citizens take a complete copy of the register as it stands at that moment.

² Both Republics succeed to that history. Neither is the continuation of the other, and neither MAY describe the other as illegitimate in its own Journal.

³ Records made after the declaration belong to the Republic that made them.

## § 3  Names, persons and holdings

¹ The departing Republic MUST take a distinct name and distinct symbols within thirty days.

² A citizen MAY hold citizenship of both, or of either, and elects within thirty days.

³ Holdings of the unit and of instruments are mirrored in both.

⁴ An office held in the Republic is not held in the departing Republic, which fills its own offices afresh.

⁵ A judgment given before the division binds both.

## § 4  Reunion

¹ Two Republics descended from a common division MAY reunite by a measure of the class of amendment carried in each.

² The terms of reunion, including the reconciliation of registers, are stated in the measure.
