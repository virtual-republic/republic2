---
id: art-08
title: Deliberation and Decision
---

## § 1  Proposal

¹ Any citizen MAY propose a measure.

² A measure MUST state its class, its text, and the provision under which it is made.

³ A measure that does not cite a resolvable provision is not received.

⁴ A measure MUST concern one subject only.

⁵ A measure MAY name the change it authorises. Where it does, it enacts that change and no other.

## § 2  Deliberation

¹ On receipt a measure is published and a period of deliberation opens.

² Any citizen MAY speak to it, and the record of deliberation is published with it.

³ The proposer MAY amend or withdraw it during deliberation.

## § 3  Voting

¹ At the close of deliberation a period of voting opens, of the length fixed for the class.

² A citizen votes by a signed ballot. A ballot not verified against a registered key is not counted.

³ One ballot is counted for each citizenship. A later ballot replaces an earlier one, and an earlier one never replaces a later.

⁴ A citizen MAY delegate their vote, generally or by class, and MAY revoke the delegation before the close. A delegated vote is exercised openly and recorded as delegated.

⁵ Voting closes at the earlier of the end of the period, or the moment the outcome can no longer be changed by the ballots not yet cast.

⁶ The outcome can no longer be changed when every citizenship has voted, when the measure would carry however the remainder were cast, or when it would fail however they were cast.

## § 4  Tally

¹ A measure carries if the ballots cast satisfy the quorum and the threshold fixed for its class.

² Quorum is reckoned against the citizenships on the register. Because one person may hold several, quorum is not a count of persons.

³ Every ballot is published at the close, with the material by which each citizen may confirm their own.

⁴ The tally MUST be reproducible by any person from the published ballots.

⁵ The tally is performed by the published tool, and the tool's result is the result.

## § 5  Enactment

¹ A measure that carries is enacted by publication in the Journal.

² Enactment does not take effect for the period fixed by statute, during which the Court MAY halt it.

³ A measure that enacts standing law produces a statute, which is the text in force.

## § 6  Elections

¹ An office is filled by the single transferable vote in its instant-runoff form.

² The rounds of elimination are published.

³ An election that carries installs its winner, whose term runs from the declaration of the result.

## § 7  Change to the tools

¹ A change to the tools, the settings or the procedure of the Republic is a measure of the class the settings fix for it.

² Such a change is given effect only after the holder of the power to approve has confirmed that what is being made effective is what carried.
