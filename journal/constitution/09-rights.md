---
id: art-09
title: Rights
entrenched: true
---

*These bind the Republic and are enforced by the Court. They are not reducible
to a rule executed by a tool, and MUST NOT be so reduced.*

## § 1  Dignity and equality

¹ Every citizen is equal in the Republic and enjoys its equal regard.

² No citizen MAY be discriminated against on any ground listed in Article 3 § 2 ⁵.

## § 2  Participation

¹ Every citizen has the right to speak in the Assembly, to propose, to vote, to stand for office and to form an entity.

² No citizen MAY be excluded from the register, the Journal or any public part of the Republic.

## § 3  Expression

¹ Every citizen MAY freely express and receive opinions and information, being answerable for the abuse of that freedom.

² The Republic MUST NOT remove a published record on account of the opinion it contains.

³ This section does not protect harassment, threats, deliberate falsehood about a person, or content unlawful where the records are held.

## § 4  Process

¹ No citizen MAY be removed, sanctioned or deprived of a power except by a reasoned decision, after notice and an opportunity to answer.

² Every citizen has the right of access to the Court and to an effective remedy.

## § 5  Property and holdings

¹ No account MAY be frozen or debited except as Article 2 § 5 ³ allows.

² An instrument or holding is not taken except by order of the Court, on stated reasons.

## § 6  Privacy

¹ The Republic collects of a person only what is necessary, and only for a stated purpose.

² Personal data is held apart from the register, and the register names a person only by their identifier.

³ Every query against a person's data is itself recorded and visible to that person.

## § 7  Contract

¹ Citizens and entities MAY contract with one another.

² A contract takes effect when every party has signed it, and not before.

³ A signature covers the text as it then stands; an alteration afterwards voids every signature given.

## § 8  Erasure

¹ Every person MAY require the erasure of their personal data.

² Erasure removes the link between the person and their identifier. It removes no record, and Article 2 § 2 is not thereby breached.

## § 9  Exit

¹ Every citizen MAY depart, and MAY take a complete copy of the register.

² No provision of this Constitution and no statute MAY impede a departure or condition it on anything.

## § 10  Decision by machine

¹ No right in this Article is denied, and no citizen is sanctioned, by the operation of a tool alone.

² A citizen affected by an automated refusal MAY require that it be stated in words, with the provision relied on, and MAY put the question to the Court.

³ A tool that refuses an act MUST say which provision it refuses under.

## § 11  Limitation

¹ A right in this Article MAY be limited only by statute, only so far as necessary in a self-governing association, and only proportionately.

² The burden of justifying a limitation lies on the organ asserting it.

³ No limitation MAY negate the essential content of a right.

⁴ Sections 9 and 10 admit of no limitation.
