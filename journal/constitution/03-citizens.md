---
id: art-03
title: Citizens
---

## § 1  Person and citizen

¹ A person is a natural human being.

² A citizen is a person admitted under this Article and not since departed.

³ A citizenship is identified by a pseudonymous identifier and by a public key.

⁴ The identifier is permanent. The key MAY be rotated.

## § 2  Admission

¹ Any person of sixteen years or more MAY apply, whether or not they already hold a citizenship.

² An application is a record adding the applicant to the register, signed by the key the citizenship will hold.

³ Admission takes effect on the recording of the application. No support, sponsorship or seconding is required.

⁴ The Registrar MAY object within the period fixed by statute, and the Assembly then decides.

⁵ Admission MUST NOT be refused on grounds of nationality, residence, ancestry, ethnicity, sex, gender, sexual orientation, religion, belief, disability, age above the minimum, or language.

## § 3  Keys

¹ A citizen acts by signing. A signature verified against a registered key is the act of that citizen.

² A citizen MAY rotate a key by a record signed with the key replaced.

³ A citizen SHOULD register a second key, held apart from the first.

⁴ Where every key is lost, the citizen MAY be restored by the Registrar on such proof as statute requires.

## § 4  Departure

¹ A citizen MAY depart at any time by a signed record, and MAY return by the procedure for admission.

² A citizen MAY be removed only by judgment of the Court, on grounds fixed by statute, and only for conduct.

³ Departure and removal alter no record already made.

⁴ A citizenship that is not active counts toward no quorum, casts no ballot and holds no office.
