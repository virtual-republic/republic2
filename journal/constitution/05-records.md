id: art-05
title: Records and the Journal
---

## § 1  The register

¹ The Republic keeps one repository holding its Constitution, its statutes, its registers, its Journal and its ledger.

² The repository is authoritative. Any other rendering of the Republic, including any website, is a client and is not authoritative.

³ The repository SHALL be public, and every citizen MAY hold a complete copy.

⁴ The Republic SHALL keep the repository on not fewer than two independent hosts.

## § 2  The Journal

¹ The Republic publishes a Journal in which every act of the Republic appears.

² Publication in the Journal is promulgation. An act not published has no effect.

³ An issue records the act and states where the text in force is kept. It does not reproduce that text.

⁴ Issues are numbered consecutively and are never withdrawn.

## § 3  Openness

¹ Every record, register, charter, judgment and tally is public.

² A record MAY be withheld only where statute so provides, only to protect a person, and only for a stated period.

³ The fact of a withholding is itself published.

## § 4  Verification

¹ The Republic SHALL publish the tools by which its register may be verified, under a licence permitting any use.

² Any person MAY operate a monitor, checking each published checkpoint against the last.

³ The Republic SHALL NOT require permission, registration or identification to verify.

⁴ The tools are the procedure. A change to them is a change to what the Republic does, and is made only as Article 8 provides.
