---

## § 1  Constitution of the Court

¹ The Court consists of one or more Judges, elected under Article 8.

² Where no Judge is elected, the Assembly exercises the functions of the Court.

## § 2  Jurisdiction

¹ The Court decides disputes arising under this Constitution.

² It reviews acts for consistency with this Constitution.

³ It construes this Constitution, the statutes and the charters.

## § 3  Powers

¹ The Court MAY halt an act within the period between its carrying and its taking effect.

² The Court MAY declare an act of no effect.

³ The Court MAY order a remedy for an infringement of Article 9.

⁴ The Court MUST NOT transfer value, and holds no power over the Treasury.

## § 4  Procedure

¹ Any citizen MAY bring a case, stating the act complained of, the ground, and what is sought.

² A case MUST cite the provisions to be construed, and those citations MUST resolve.

³ Every judgment is published, cites the provisions construed, and states reasons.

⁴ A person affected has the right to be heard and to appeal as statute provides.
