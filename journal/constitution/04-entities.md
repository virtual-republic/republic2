id: art-04
title: Entities
---

## § 1  Formation

¹ Every citizen MAY form an entity of a type the settings mark as formed by right. No permission is required.

² An entity of a type marked as formed by law exists only on a carried measure, and is entered by the Registrar citing that measure.

³ Formation is a record stating the type, the charter, the organs and the founding members.

## § 2  Capacity

¹ An entity has the capacities its type is given and no others.

² An entity MAY hold an account, hold property of the Republic, employ organs and appear before the Court.

³ An entity of a type so marked MAY issue instruments representing a share in itself.

⁴ An entity MUST NOT be a citizen, MUST NOT vote and MUST NOT hold an office of the Republic.

## § 3  Charter and organs

¹ Every entity has a charter, which is a published document.

² An organ is held by one or more citizens and holds only the authority the charter confers.

³ A charter MUST NOT be inconsistent with this Constitution.

⁴ An entity acts only through an organ of it.

## § 4  Dissolution

¹ An entity is dissolved by its charter's procedure, by resolution of its members, or by judgment of the Court.

² On dissolution its holdings pass as the charter provides, and failing that to the Treasury.
