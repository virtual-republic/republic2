## § 1

¹ A Commune is a political body of citizens and is a legal person.

² The Charter of a Commune MAY be a fork of the repository but MAY edit the details of the repository, except entrenched provisions of the Constitution MUST remain in the Charter.

³ Communes MUST follow all law.

⁴ Communes MAY declare independence unilaterally from the Republic.

## § 2

¹ The Commune of the Bay Area is established.

² As construed under const.art-04/§3/¶4, the Council of the Bay Area is the executive organ of the Commune of the Bay Area.
