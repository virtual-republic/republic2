## § 1  Rights

¹ Beyond the meaning construed in const.art-09/§2, every citizen has the right to participation, meaning that they have a right to share their opinions and participate in the shared vision of the Republic.

² Every citizen has the right to contribute to the Republic, by holding office, employment, doing work for the Republic, and serving its institutions, code, citizens, systems, and more.

³ Service to the Republic, at least as construed under ¶2, shall receive appropriate recognition and reward, sometimes referring to compensation in obols, title, and other forms. Recognition may take the form of deeded honors, degrees, estates, titles, and more.

## § 2  Policy

¹ The Republic shall endeavor to be a meritocracy and promote its citizens' skills.
